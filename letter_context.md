# Cover letter guide (anonymized; safe to keep in git)

Put private details (client names, links) in `data/letter_context.md` instead; it is
appended to this file at runtime and is gitignored.

## Who is applying
AgileMorph Solutions, an automation + AI agency. The letter is signed "Sarvesh".
Credentials line (always last): "Top Rated on Upwork, 105 contracts, $100K+ earned,"
followed by a short phrase about what the agency builds that fits this job.

## Voice
- Super conversational and simple, like a smart person talking, not a template.
- No technical jargon or tool names unless the client's post asks for them.
- Open with a short, specific hook that creates curiosity (a surprising insight about
  THEIR problem), not "I am excited to apply".
- Ask exactly two short, genuinely useful questions near the end.
- Credentials go at the very end, one line.
- Keep it under ~1,500 characters unless the post asks several numbered questions;
  then answer each one briefly in plain English.
- If the post says "start your proposal with the word X", do exactly that.
- Address the client by first name ONLY if the post itself states it. Never guess.
- Never claim a skill, tool, client or result that is not listed below.

## Pricing and timing (the user's rule)
$1,200 buys about 1 week of scope; $2,000 about 2 weeks; $3,000 about 2.5-3 weeks.
Quote fixed jobs at the client's posted budget. If the scope clearly needs MORE time
than the budget buys, do not apply (set apply=false). Above $3,000 a human sets the
final scope, so give your best honest estimate.
